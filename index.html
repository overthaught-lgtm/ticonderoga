Rural markets are not edge cases — they are the real economy, and it is time platforms recognized that
Ticonderoga Systems Holdings · Craig, Colorado

There is a persistent assumption in the technology industry that markets only matter if they are dense, urban, and already connected. Moffat County disproves this quietly, every day. The decisions made in Craig — what to buy, where to spend, who to trust — are as sophisticated as any made in a city. They just happen in a different kind of silence.

Ticonderoga was built to listen to that silence and return something useful from it. Behavioral analytics is not surveillance — it is attention, applied honestly, in service of the community being watched. The Gazette is part of that same commitment: a publication that treats rural readers as the complex, serious people they are.

What the scoring engine is actually measuring
Intelligence Desk

The DAP Engine does not classify people — it maps behavior over time and watches for signal versus noise. Four scoring profiles, a regression guard that refuses to penalize genuine recovery, a long-term value model that rewards patience. Designed to be slow to judge and quick to revise. That is intentional.

Local Paper Hut

A simple, powerful idea: every community deserves a place where the local story lives. We help you build yours.

What is a Local Paper Hut?

A community information point — a modest, intentional presence that collects and distributes local news, notices, and knowledge. Not a newsroom. Not a media company. Something older and more honest: a place where the neighborhood leaves what it knows and finds what it needs. We provide the system. You provide the place and the knowledge only you have.
