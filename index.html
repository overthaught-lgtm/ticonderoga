Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What hardware do I need?
Simple sensor arrays that plug into standard outlets. No special infrastructure or IT support required. Most locations are operational within 48 hours.
Is customer data ever shared?
Never. All processing happens on-premises. We never see or access your customer data. Our TEAKWOOD protocol ensures complete data sovereignty.
How long does deployment take?
Most locations are operational within 48 hours of hardware delivery. Multi-location deployments typically complete within 2-3 weeks.
What support do you provide?
Dedicated account managers, 24/7 system monitoring, quarterly business reviews, and priority support for all customers.
Can I try it before buying?
Yes. We offer a 30-day pilot program with full support. Contact us to schedule a demo and pilot deployment.
What integrations are available?
REST API, webhooks, and native integrations with Salesforce, Shopify, HubSpot, Google Analytics, Slack, and Power BI.
